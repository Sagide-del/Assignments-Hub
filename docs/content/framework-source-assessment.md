For example, printed pages 39–40 discuss middle-school outcomes and upper-primary learning areas. Its secondary-education section groups Grades 7–9 and Grades 10–12.

This can support proposed subject structure, broad learning goals and constraints for AI-authored draft activities. It is not a complete current subject-by-subject Grade 6–12 design containing every strand, sub-strand and grade-specific learning outcome. Its date and historical learning-area organisation must remain visible. It does not establish the current examination format or certify complete present-day coverage.

The existing ingestion classification should retain these as `FRAMEWORK`, not silently promote them to detailed `CURRICULUM` designs. AI-proposed detailed outcomes are not source quotations. The current verified-outcome generator still needs reviewed grade/subject curriculum evidence; these framework uploads alone do not populate all missing detailed outcomes or publish learner content.
