STEM Pathways in Kenyan High Schools: A Parent’s Guide

Assignment Hub Team · Updated 2026-11-16

The short answer

KICD’s 2019 framework groups STEM into Pure Sciences, Applied Sciences, Technical and Engineering, and Career and Technology Studies (CTS). Confirm current school combinations and selection rules on the MoE portal before choosing for Grade 10.

Key takeaways

  • Compare interests with actual school subject combinations.
  • Birth order is not a career-selection test.

What the Research Says

Rohrer and colleagues found no lasting birth-order effects on extraversion or conscientiousness in US, UK and German panels. The study did not establish career suitability; being first-born is not evidence that a child should enter management.

  • Rohrer, Egloff & Schmukle (2015), PNAS

How This Connects to Your Child’s Career

Subject combinations can affect later course eligibility. Use Career Compass to organise the conversation, then confirm current requirements with institutions.
