The short answer

Core Mathematics commonly supports STEM routes; Essential Mathematics serves other pathway needs. Confirm current designs and entry requirements: neither label alone establishes university eligibility.

What the Research Says

Eccles and Wigfield reviewed how expectations of success and perceived task value influence achievement-related choices. Ask whether a difficulty reflects a missing skill, low confidence or an unclear purpose. These findings inform support rather than measure fixed aptitude.

  • Eccles & Wigfield (2002), Annual Review of Psychology

How This Connects to Your Child’s Career

Mathematics requirements differ by course. Verify current institutional requirements before narrowing STEM, Business or Arts options.
