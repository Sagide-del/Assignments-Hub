RESPIRATORY ANATOMY ATLAS SUBSET

This directory's respiratory-atlas.glb contains a respiratory subset of Z-Anatomy,
The Libre 3D Atlas of Anatomy, by Gauthier Kervyn (design, 3D, anatomy) and
Marcin Zielinski (Blender script), derived from BodyParts3D by Kousaku Okubo,
The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS).

Source: https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy
Source commit: 6ffffec1d9b17ed3db355903bbd762582cb2328e
Source archive: Z-Anatomy.zip / Z-Anatomy/Startup.blend

Extracted structures: five lung lobes, trachea, main/lobar/segmental bronchi and
 diaphragm (35 named meshes). This subset does not include the separately
credited kidney, inner-ear or cranial-nerve assets listed in the upstream notice.

Assignment Hub modifications: extraction of the respiratory subset, conversion
of airway curves to meshes, removal of atlas labels and excess modifiers,
smoothing/subdivision of lung-lobe surfaces, glTF export, display materials and
illustrative animated deformation. The anatomy mesh adaptations and rendered
atlas previews are offered under CC BY-SA 4.0. Attribution must be retained and
adapted model data shared under the same license. The source atlas and this
adaptation are not an official endorsement of Assignment Hub.

The original application code is separate from the licensed anatomy asset.
The alveolar close-up is an original schematic, not a patient-derived mesh.
Breathing deformation and ventilation calculations are educational models,
not patient-specific medical measurements or a diagnostic device.

Reproducible export script: frontend/scripts/assets/export-respiratory-atlas.py
Model integrity and structure list: manifest.json
