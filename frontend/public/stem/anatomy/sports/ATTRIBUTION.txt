The muscle, energy and neural pathways are original teaching schematics and do not constitute measured physiological data.

arm-muscles-atlas-v1.glb: right biceps (long and short heads), triceps
(three heads), brachialis, scapula, humerus, radius and ulna, from the same
pinned Z-Anatomy atlas revision. CC BY-SA 4.0. Adapted by selecting a subset,
reducing dense meshes and converting to glTF; illustrative materials applied
in the viewer. Geometry is a resting anatomical model, not a simulated elbow.
Reproduction script: frontend/scripts/assets/export-muscle-atlas.py.
Checksums and modification details: arm-muscles-manifest.json.

energy-atlas-v1.glb, reflex-atlas-v1.glb and circulation-atlas-v1.glb:
Z-Anatomy / BodyParts3D subsets, CC BY-SA 4.0, same upstream revision and
credits above. Adaptations: named tissue subsets, curve-to-surface conversion
for nerves and blood vessels, polygon reduction, smooth normals and glTF
export. Illustrative viewer colours do not change anatomical geometry.
Exporter: frontend/scripts/assets/export-sports-systems.py.
Checksums, sources, modifications and included structures: systems-manifest.json.
