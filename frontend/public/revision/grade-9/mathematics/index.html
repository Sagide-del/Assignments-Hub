1. A school kitchen pays KES 540 for 3 kg of rice. At the same price per kilogram, what will 5 kg cost?

2. Solve the equation 3(2x − 1) = 21.

3. A rectangular garden is 6 m wide and 8 m long. What is the straight-line distance between opposite corners?

4. A closed cylindrical container has radius 7 cm and height 10 cm. Using π = 22/7, what is its volume?

5. A bag contains 3 red, 2 blue and 5 green counters. One counter is picked at random. What is the probability that it is blue?

Answers and explanations

1. A school kitchen pays KES 540 for 3 kg of rice. At the same price per kilogram, what will 5 kg cost?

Answer: KES 900.

One kilogram costs 540 ÷ 3 = KES 180. Therefore 5 kg costs 5 × 180 = KES 900. The same unit price must be used for both quantities.

2. Solve the equation 3(2x − 1) = 21.

Answer: x = 4.

Expand the bracket: 6x − 3 = 21. Add 3 to both sides to get 6x = 24. Divide by 6, so x = 4. Check: 3(8 − 1) = 21.

3. A rectangular garden is 6 m wide and 8 m long. What is the straight-line distance between opposite corners?

Answer: 10 m.

The diagonal is the hypotenuse of a right-angled triangle. Its square is 6² + 8² = 36 + 64 = 100, so the diagonal is √100 = 10 m.

4. A closed cylindrical container has radius 7 cm and height 10 cm. Using π = 22/7, what is its volume?

Answer: 1,540 cm³.

Volume = πr²h = (22/7) × 7 × 7 × 10 = 1,540 cm³. Radius is squared; the volume is measured in cubic centimetres, not square centimetres.

5. A bag contains 3 red, 2 blue and 5 green counters. One counter is picked at random. What is the probability that it is blue?

Answer: 1/5.

There are 3 + 2 + 5 = 10 counters altogether, and 2 are blue. The probability is favourable outcomes ÷ total outcomes = 2/10 = 1/5.
