Before you start

  • Use a common denominator before adding or subtracting fractions.
  • Simplify by dividing the numerator and denominator by the same factor.
  • To find a fraction of a quantity, divide by the denominator, then multiply by the numerator.

Answers and explanations

Open a question to see the answer and the steps behind it.

1. Which fraction is equivalent to 3/4?

Answer: 6/8. Multiply both the numerator and denominator by 2: 3/4 = 6/8. The value stays the same.

2. What is 1/3 + 1/6 in its simplest form?

Answer: 1/2. Use denominator 6. 1/3 = 2/6, so 2/6 + 1/6 = 3/6 = 1/2.

3. A learner reads 3/5 of a 40-page booklet. How many pages have they read?

Answer: 24. One fifth of 40 is 8. Three fifths is 3 × 8 = 24 pages.

4. What is 5/6 − 1/4 in its simplest form?

Answer: 7/12. Use denominator 12: 5/6 = 10/12 and 1/4 = 3/12. Subtract to get 7/12.

5. Which fraction is the largest?

Answer: 3/4. With denominator 24 the fractions are 16/24, 18/24, 15/24 and 12/24. Therefore 3/4 is largest.
