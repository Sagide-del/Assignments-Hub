Our learning standard

Learning support, never assignment outsourcing.

Assignment Hub helps Kenyan learners practise, receive feedback, build practical skills and complete teacher-issued work honestly.

We do not write, complete, or sell student assignments, essays, projects, or examination responses.

What we provide

Original practice questions, auto-marked assessments, revision guides, study tools, practical STEM activities and school-approved learning resources.

What learners do

Learners complete their own work. Feedback explains errors and recommends useful next practice rather than replacing thinking or teacher guidance.

What schools control

Schools choose their assignments, review written and practical evidence, and can keep school-created resources private.
